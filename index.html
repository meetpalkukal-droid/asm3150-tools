A growing set of web-based tools to help students in ASM 3150/5150 work through irrigation and water management calculations. New tools will be added throughout the semester — bookmark this page.

Chapter 2 — Soil Water

⚖️

Soil Bulk Density & Porosity

Solve ρb = Ms/Vb and find porosity — useful for compaction checks and water storage capacity.

 💧

Soil Water Content & Irrigation Depth

From a soil sample to mass and volumetric water content to an equivalent depth — and how much more water is needed.

 📅

Soil Water Depletion & Irrigation Scheduling

Find fraction depleted, fraction remaining, available water capacity, and soil water deficit for a root zone.

 🌊

Infiltration Depth & Deep Percolation

Find how deep an irrigation application's wetting front penetrates a layered soil, and how much is lost below the root zone.

 🧭

Soil Texture Water Characteristics

Look up planning-level field capacity, wilting point, and available water capacity by soil texture (Table 2.3).

Chapter 3 — Measuring Water Applications

📐

Unit Converter

Convert between USCS and SI units for volume, flow, length, and area used in water measurement.

 🪣

Depth – Volume – Area

Solve d = V/A for depth, volume, or area applied — including meter totalizer readings.

 ⏱️

Flow – Time – Depth – Area

Solve Q·t = A·d for flow rate, time, area, or depth of application.

 🌀

Pipe Flow (Continuity)

Solve Q = Vm·A for flow rate, mean velocity, or pipe diameter.
