About this calculator

Bundles three related checks from Chapter 7 (Salinity Management) that a grower would naturally run together after a water test comes back: how much salt that water adds to the field (Eq. 5.18), what it will do to a given crop's yield if the root zone reaches that salinity (Eq. 7.3, Table 7.2), and whether the water's sodium content is likely to hurt infiltration (Eq. 7.4, Figure 7.10).

Figure 7.10's two boundary lines (Rhoades, 1982) have no printed equation in the textbook — they were digitized directly from the published figure (sub-pixel least-squares line fits against a 400 DPI page render) rather than approximated, to stay faithful to the book's own chart rather than a generic substitute.

Worked examples this bundles: Example 7.1 (salt load from a 500 mg/L water applied at 15 in to 50 ac → 42 tons), Example 7.2 (corn at 3,000 mg/L → 64% relative yield, 115 bu/ac from a 180 bu/ac potential), and Example 7.3 (SAR=8.2 at EC=0.4 dS/m → infiltration likely reduced). Defaults above reproduce all three.
