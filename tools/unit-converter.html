Water Measurement Unit Converter

Convert between U.S. Customary (USCS) and International System (SI) units commonly used in irrigation and water management: volume, flow, length, and area.

Converter

—

Water Weight Equivalence

Applies to water only, at standard conditions (1 gal water ≈ 8.33 lb; 1 L water ≈ 1 kg). Useful for converting an applied water volume to a mass, e.g. for fertigation calculations.
