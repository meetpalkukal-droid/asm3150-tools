About this calculator

Bundles the basic energy/continuity relationships (Eq. 8.1-8.10) with friction loss (Hazen-Williams, Eq. 8.11, Table 8.1) and minor losses (Eq. 8.12, Table 8.4) into the practical end-to-end question a designer actually asks: given an inlet pressure, what pressure comes out the other end of this pipe?

Worked examples this bundles: Section 1's defaults reproduce Example 8.2/8.3 (total/velocity head at two points on either side of a 4-in to 10-in transition, 400 gpm). Sections 2-4 default to Example 8.4 (a 1280-ft, 4-in aluminum lateral with 33 sprinklers at 5 gpm each, rising 12 ft, 60 psi inlet → 49.7 psi at the end) — switch Section 2 to "Single pipe run" mode to size an ordinary pipe segment instead of a lateral.

Note: the textbook states 33 sprinklers on a 1280-ft lateral at 40-ft spacing, but 1280÷40 = 32 by Table 8.3's own convention (first outlet one full spacing from the inlet). This tool enters outlet count and lateral length as two independent numbers (both matching the book's own stated values) rather than deriving one from the other, so it reproduces the example exactly either way — use the "outlets × spacing" button only when you want the length derived instead of entered directly.
