About this calculator

Net system capacity (Cn) is the rate of water supply needed to prevent crop water stress during peak demand. Gross system capacity (Cg) scales that up to account for application inefficiency and planned system downtime.

A useful conversion: 18.86 gpm/ac ≈ 1 in/d (derived here from the same precise acre-inch and gallon factors used throughout this site, rather than the rounded constant).

Textbook Example 5.7: A sandy loam soil (AWC=1.5 in/ft), 4-ft corn root zone, 50% allowable depletion → AD = 3.0 in. From that point the textbook reads Cn ≈ 0.22 in/d off Figure 5.11's probability chart — a different (and not reproducible here) method than Table 5.3's general estimate. The AD calculation in Step 1 above matches the book exactly.
