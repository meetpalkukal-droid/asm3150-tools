About this calculator

Bundles the three practical "how much extra water" questions from Chapter 7 (Salinity Management): whether past irrigation leached enough to prevent salt buildup (Eq. 7.6/7.7, diagnostic), how much extra water a crop and water quality require going forward (Eq. 7.7 + Figure 7.11, planning), and how much water it takes to reclaim an already salt-affected soil (Eq. 7.9/7.10).

Figure 7.11 (Hoffman & van Genuchten, 1983) has no printed equation — every Lr curve on it is a straight line through the origin, so it was digitized as leaching requirement vs. the ratio T/ECa (sub-pixel least-squares fits against a 400 DPI page render). The digitization was checked against the book's own Example 7.5 (ECa=2.4, T=2.5 → Lr=0.17): this model gives 0.175, and the surrounding 0.15/0.20 curves correctly bracket it.

Worked examples this bundles: Example 7.4 (300 mm irrigation + 250 mm rain, Ci=0.5, Cd=2.5 → Lf=0.11), Example 7.5 (tomatoes, ECi=3, 16 in + 4 in rain → Lr≈0.17), and Example 7.6 (3 ft clay loam, ECe 10→2 dS/m → 4.5 ft ponded or 1.5 ft sprinkled). Defaults above reproduce all three.
