About this calculator

Bundles the energy-side of pump selection from Chapter 8 (Examples 8.6-8.10) into one chain: total dynamic head → power requirement → how this plant's actual fuel or electricity use compares to the Nebraska Pumping Plant Performance Criteria (Table 8.6, all five energy sources built in) → the energy cost of every acre-inch pumped, with an optional price per unit for a $ figure the book doesn't compute but a manager would want.

Worked examples this bundles: Example 8.6 (800 gpm, 50 psi, 100 ft lift, 80% efficient → 44 whp, 55 bhp), Example 8.7 (4 gal/hr diesel measured → performance rating 0.88), Example 8.8 (what consumption would be at PR=1 → 3.52 gal/hr), and Examples 8.9/8.10 (2.24 gal/ac-in at the measured rating, 1.97 gal/ac-in if improved to PR=1). Defaults above reproduce all five.

Note: this tool carries full precision through every step rather than rounding TDH to a whole number the way the book's worked solution does for readability. Eq. 8.13 gives TDH = 2.31×50+100 = 215.5 ft exactly; the book rounds this to 216 ft before continuing, so whp/bhp/PR/E downstream of it differ from the printed answers by about 1% — a rounding-path difference, not a different formula. Example 8.10 (PR=1) happens to land almost exactly on the book's 1.97 regardless.
