About this calculator

For a full, circular pipe flowing under pressure, the continuity equation relates flow rate (Q), mean velocity (Vm), and the pipe's cross-sectional flow area (Af):

Q = Vm × Af   where   Af = (π/4) × D²

Pick which quantity you want to solve for, enter the other two, and choose the unit you'd like the answer reported in. This is useful for sizing pipe (given a target flow and a maximum allowable velocity), checking whether an existing pipeline can carry a design flow, or converting a measured velocity to a flow rate.

Textbook Example 3.3: An 8-in ID pipeline has a mean velocity of 5 ft/s. Flow rate ≈ 785 gpm (the textbook rounds 1 cfs ≈ 450 gpm; this calculator uses precise conversion factors, so it will show a very close value). Click "Load textbook Example 3.3" above to see it worked through.
