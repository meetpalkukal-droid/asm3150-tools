About this calculator

System capacity has to account for more than just crop demand: application inefficiency and downtime inflate the net capacity into a gross capacity, and then each field's own conveyance losses (and any shared main-canal losses) inflate that further into what the well actually has to discharge.

Qf, field = Cg × Area / (Ec, field/100%)   —   Qwell = ΣQf / (Ec, main/100%)

Textbook Example 5.8: Cn=0.3 in/d, ELQ=65%, Dt=10% → Cg≈9.7 gpm/ac. Two 80-ac furrow fields, field efficiencies 80% and 90%, main canal at 90% → Qf1≈975 gpm, Qf2≈867 gpm, well discharge ≈2,050 gpm. Click "Load textbook Example 5.8" above to see it worked through.
