About this calculator

Available water capacity (AWC) is the depth of water per unit soil depth that's actually usable by the plant — held between field capacity and the wilting point. As the crop uses water, the fraction depleted (fd) climbs from 0 (at field capacity) toward 1 (at the wilting point). Soil water deficit (SWD) is the depth of water needed to refill the root zone back to field capacity — i.e., how much to irrigate right now.

AWC = θfc − θwp   —   fd = (θfc − θv) / AWC   —   SWD = (θfc − θv) × L   —   TAW = AWC × L

Textbook Example 2.3: A silt loam soil (θfc = 0.34, θwp = 0.16) with a current water content of 0.26, over a 36-in root zone: fd = 0.44, fr = 0.56, AWC = 0.18 in/in, SWD ≈ 2.9 in. Click "Load textbook Example 2.3" above to see it worked through.
