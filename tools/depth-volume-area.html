About this calculator

Depth of application (d), volume of water applied (V), and area irrigated (A) are related by:

d = V / A   —   V = A × d   —   A = V / d

Pick which quantity you want to solve for, enter the other two, and choose the unit you'd like the answer reported in. If your volume comes from a flow meter's volume totalizer, check "Use flow meter totalizer readings" and enter the before and after readings directly — the calculator subtracts them for you.

Textbook Example 3.2: A flow meter totalizer registered 12,590,900 gal after an irrigation and 8,925,100 gal before it, on a 90-acre field. Depth applied = 1.5 in. Click "Load textbook Example 3.2" above to see it worked through.
