Soil Moisture Sensor Irrigation Scheduling

Turns a set of soil moisture sensor readings into an irrigation decision — when to irrigate and how much — the way a grower or crop consultant actually uses a probe with sensors at a few depths. Works with any number of sensors at any depths; nothing here depends on looking up a soil type or crop from a table — every parameter (field capacity, available water capacity, allowable depletion) is something you enter directly from your own sensor calibration, lab data, or best estimate.

About this calculator

Each sensor's reading is assumed to represent the soil halfway to its neighboring sensors — the standard "midpoint method" for turning point readings into a profile total (see USU Extension's soil sensor setup guide). The shallowest sensor's zone extends up to the surface; the deepest sensor's zone extends down to the root zone depth you set above.

thickness_i = midpoint(d_(i-1), d_i) to midpoint(d_i, d_(i+1))

Multiplying each sensor's water content by its zone's thickness and summing gives the total water in the profile at that moment:

D_total = sum(theta_i x thickness_i)

The trigger (refill point) is the field-capacity total minus the allowable depletion; once the current total drops to or below it, it's time to irrigate. The depth to apply is simply what's needed to bring the profile back to field capacity:

Refill point = FC - MAD   —   Depth to apply = FC - D_current

A technical note on precision: the absolute profile totals (field capacity, current water) depend on exactly how zone thicknesses are assigned, so two reasonable conventions can give slightly different absolute numbers. The differences that actually drive the schedule — depth to apply, and remaining buffer before the trigger — are not sensitive to that choice, as long as it's applied consistently to every reading (verified directly against the worked example below).

Worked example (class slide): A 2-ft root zone in a silt loam soil, sensors at 6, 12, and 24 in. After a heavy spring rain (field capacity): 32%, 29%, 34%. Current readings: 24%, 27%, 29%. AWC ≈ 2.1 in/ft (4.2 in total), MAD = 50% → allowable depletion = 2.1 in. Depth needed to return to field capacity ≈ 1.2 in; remaining usable water before the trigger ≈ 0.9 in.
