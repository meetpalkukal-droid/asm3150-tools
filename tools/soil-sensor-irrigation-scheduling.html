Soil Moisture Sensor Irrigation Scheduling

Turns a set of soil moisture sensor readings into an irrigation decision — when to irrigate and how much — the way a grower or crop consultant actually uses a probe with sensors at a few depths. Works with any number of sensors at any depths. Field capacity and wilting point are both things you can enter directly from your own sensor calibration or lab data — and if you don't know the wilting point, there's a soil-texture estimate as a fallback, but nothing here requires it.

About this calculator

Each sensor's reading is assumed to represent the soil halfway to its neighboring sensors — the standard "midpoint method" for turning point readings into a profile total (see USU Extension's soil sensor setup guide). The shallowest sensor's zone extends up to the surface; the deepest sensor's zone extends down to the root zone depth you set above.

thickness_i = midpoint(d_(i-1), d_i) to midpoint(d_i, d_(i+1))

Multiplying each sensor's water content by its zone's thickness and summing gives the total water in the profile at that moment — done once for field capacity and once for wilting point, with available water capacity simply the difference:

D_total = sum(theta_i x thickness_i); AWC = FC - PWP

The trigger (refill point) is the field-capacity total minus the allowable depletion; once the current total drops to or below it, it's time to irrigate. The depth to apply is simply what's needed to bring the profile back to field capacity:

Refill point = FC - MAD   —   Depth to apply = FC - D_current

A technical note on precision: the absolute profile totals (field capacity, current water) depend on exactly how zone thicknesses are assigned, so two reasonable conventions can give slightly different absolute numbers. The differences that actually drive the schedule — depth to apply, and remaining buffer before the trigger — are not sensitive to that choice, as long as it's applied consistently to every reading.

For example: a 2-ft root zone with sensors at 6, 12, and 24 in, reading 32%, 29%, 34% at field capacity and 24%, 27%, 29% right now, with a 50% MAD, works out to a depth of about 1.2 in needed to refill the profile, with about 0.9 in of buffer remaining before the trigger point.
