About this calculator

Built around the Flowserve 12SKL/12KSL curve from Figure 8.11 — but instead of reproducing just the four printed diameter curves, this tool digitized one reference curve (8.19 in, 1770 rpm; a handful of points, quadratic fit, anchored to this chapter's own Example 8.11 point of 57 ft/stage at 800 gpm) and generates every other diameter or speed from it using the affinity laws (Eq. 8.15a/b) — the same relationship manufacturers use to produce trim curves in the first place. Checked against Example 8.11's own chart-read answers: this model's trim comes out to about 7.66 in (book: 7.75 in) and its VFD speed to about 1656 rpm (book: 1670 rpm), both within about 1%. Efficiency is an approximate peaked curve calibrated from two labeled points on the reference curve, not a digitization of the full efficiency contour map — treat it as indicative.

Worked example this reproduces: Example 8.11 — 800 gpm at 40 psi, 143 ft lift, 5-stage 12SKL with an 8.19-in impeller at 1770 rpm. The installed pump is oversized (TDH≈285 ft vs. 235 ft needed); the correct fix is trimming to ~7.7 in or slowing to ~1660 rpm, and a throttling valve instead wastes about 21% more energy per acre-inch.
